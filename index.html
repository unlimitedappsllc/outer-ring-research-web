03 · Method

How a run works

One engagement, five steps, nothing hidden.

1Brief

We agree the product, the flows that matter, and what a pass looks like.

2Full-team run

All three squads use the real, live product end to end — including checkout where it applies.

3Session reports

You receive what each tester did and said, in their own words — the evidence, not a digest of it.

4Roll-up verdict

CLEARED, FIXES REQUIRED, or HELD. Nothing is called clear until it is fixed.

5Full cost accounting

Every engagement closes with a complete accounting of what the run cost and what it covered.

The verdict scale

Every engagement closes on exactly one of three verdicts. The scale is fixed — it does not bend to a launch date.

CLEAREDThe full-team run is complete and the product is cleared on the evidence of the session reports.
FIXES REQUIREDFindings stand between the product and a clear verdict. Nothing is called clear until it is fixed.
HELDA HELD verdict blocks a launch recommendation until the blocking findings are fixed and re-proven.
On every delivered report

The seal on your report is the verdict you can stand behind

Every delivered report carries the Outer Ring seal — the mark that a full-team run happened on the real product, that the findings are the testers' own words, and that the verdict was earned, not assigned. A HELD verdict blocks a launch recommendation until the blocking findings are fixed and re-proven.

04 · B2B Testing

B2B testing: departments and the chains between them

A B2B product is not used by lone users screen by screen — it is used by an organization. So we test it as an organization: department seats, and the handoffs that cross them, because B2B failures live at the handoffs — permissions, approvals, and data passing between roles.

Department seats

  • Owner / Executive
  • Admin / IT
  • Finance / Billing
  • Sales
  • Operations
  • Support / Customer Success
  • Compliance / Security
  • Frontline end users

Cross-functional chains

  • Procure → Onboard → Use → Support → Bill → Renew
  • Invite → Permissions → Collaborate → Audit
  • Lead → Quote → Contract → Deliver → Invoice

A chain passes only if every handoff lands. B2B verdicts report department coverage and chain results alongside individual sessions.
