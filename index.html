02 · Verdict

Every engagement ends in a verdict

The verdict scale

Every engagement closes on exactly one of three verdicts. The scale is fixed — it does not bend to a launch date.

CLEAREDThe run is complete and the product is cleared on the evidence of the session reports.
FIXES REQUIREDFindings stand between the product and a clear verdict. Nothing is called clear until it is fixed.
HELDA HELD verdict blocks a launch recommendation until the blocking findings are fixed and re-proven.
On every delivered report

The seal on your report is the verdict you can stand behind

Every delivered report carries the Outer Ring seal — the mark that a full run happened on the real product, that the findings are the testers' own words, and that the verdict was earned, not assigned. A HELD verdict blocks a launch recommendation until the blocking findings are fixed and re-proven.

03 · B2B Testing

B2B testing: tested the way an organization uses it

A B2B product is used by an organization, not lone users screen by screen.

The Ring tests across the departments that will live in it and along the handoffs between them, because B2B failures live at the handoffs — permissions, approvals, data passing between roles.

Engagements are scoped to the client's organization, and verdicts report coverage across it.
